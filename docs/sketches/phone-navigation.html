Kaja on a phone: the sidebar as a drawer, and the tree as pages

Today the finder is both the search and the whole navigation: a modal sheet with Recent, the tree folding in place, and Kaja's rows. The sketches below take the Claude app's shape instead. A drawer at the left edge holds the top level and what you touched recently, the pane to its right is where you drill down, and every page says what it is under and how to go back. Search is a button again.

A. The drawer

An edge swipe, or the ☰ in the header, slides the drawer in and pushes the page right, still visible. It is the desktop sidebar's band, foot and two groups, one column.

Cold start. Start is the first page, with the drawer closed. It is the drawer's top level said once more as content, so a new phone has a way in without a gesture nobody has told it about.
What stays the same: every view body, the code sheet and its three stops, the run's segmented control, the compile and agent marks (they move from finder rows to drawer rows), the desktop above 640px.

What changes: the header stops being a combobox and becomes ☰ / back + title + action. The finder's sheet is split into the drawer (top level and Recent) and a search page. The app tree stops folding and becomes pages. A history stack of pages replaces "the finder opens on the previous place".
